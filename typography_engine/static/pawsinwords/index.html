📸 A photo that shines

  • Front-facing, looking at the camera
  • Their face fills most of the frame
  • Good, even light — the eyes are clear

Skip these

  • Far away or full-body shots
  • Side profiles (we lose the eyes)
  • Dark, blurry, or busy backgrounds
