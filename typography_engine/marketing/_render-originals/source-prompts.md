# Typortrait — image-generation prompts (asset record)

A record of the ChatGPT image prompts used to generate the marketing assets,
so they can be reproduced or tweaked later. Three asset types, in pipeline order:

1. **Source photo** — a plain head-and-shoulders portrait on a gray background.
   This is the *input* you upload to the Typortrait app.
2. **Render** — the word-art portrait the app produces (`use-children.*`,
   `use-memorial.*`, `use-graduate.*`).
3. **Framed mockup** — the render shown as a framed print in a room
   (`mock-children.*`, `mock-memorial.*`, `mock-graduate.*`). Generated by
   uploading the render, then pasting the mockup prompt.

## 2. Framed-mockup prompts

> Upload the matching render (`use-children.png` / `use-memorial.png` /
> `use-graduate.png`) **first**, then paste the prompt. The key instruction in
> all three keeps ChatGPT from redrawing the artwork. Run all three in one
> session so the lighting/realism stays uniform across the set.

### Children (upload `use-children.png` first)
```
Use the image I uploaded as a finished art print. Place it UNCHANGED inside a picture frame in a realistic interior photo. Do not redraw, restyle, recolor, crop, regenerate, or "improve" the artwork or its words — treat it exactly as a flat printed poster mounted behind the matt; every letter must stay legible and identical to the upload.

Frame & mount: a clean light-oak wooden frame with a wide off-white archival matt border around the artwork. Glass with subtle, realistic reflections.

Scene: the framed print hanging on a softly painted warm-white wall in a bright, tidy child's room — a hint of a wooden toy or a small green plant out of focus in the lower corner, nothing cluttered or distracting.

Photography: natural soft daylight from the side, gentle realistic shadow behind the frame, shallow depth of field so the print is crisp and the room falls away. Eye-level, straight-on, print centered. Cozy, premium, gift-worthy mood. 4:5 vertical, photorealistic.
```

Scene: the framed print resting on a wooden console or mantel against a warm neutral wall, with a single small softly-lit candle and a low green plant nearby — tasteful, calm, and respectful, never somber or cluttered.

Photography: warm low-key daylight, gentle realistic shadows, shallow depth of field with the print sharp and the background softly blurred. Eye-level, straight-on. Quiet, dignified, heartfelt mood. 4:5 vertical, photorealistic.
