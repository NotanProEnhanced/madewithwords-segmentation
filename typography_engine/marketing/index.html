Frequently asked questions

What is Typortrait?

Typortrait turns a photo into a portrait made entirely of words. You upload a photo and the words that matter — a name, traits, a date, even a whole letter — and Typortrait composes a likeness out of that text.

How does it work?

Upload a clear head-and-shoulders photo, type your words or paste a message, choose a style and colour, and Typortrait composes your word‑portrait in seconds. Then download it or order a print.

What kind of photo works best?

A clear, well‑lit, front‑facing head‑and‑shoulders photo of one person on a simple background gives the best likeness.

Can I use someone’s name or my own message?

Yes. Use a name, the traits you love, a date, or song lyrics — or paste a whole letter. In Story style your passage is laid out as readable prose that forms the face.

What styles and colours are available?

Two styles — a word mosaic and a story calligram — plus colours including navy, sepia, burgundy, forest, gold‑on‑black and full photo colour, with an optional framed keepsake layout.

Can I print it?

Yes. You get a high‑resolution download and a print‑quality vector, ideal for framing and gifting.

Is Typortrait good for gifts?

Yes — it’s made for anniversaries, weddings, memorials, birthdays, Mother’s and Father’s Day, new babies and pet portraits.

How long does it take?

Just seconds — your portrait is composed right in your browser.

What happens to my photo?

Your photo is used only to create your portrait.
