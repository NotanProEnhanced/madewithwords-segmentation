## What it is

A second pet renderer, `app/pet_v2/`, reached from the same call sites as the first through
`pet_proto.render_pet_portrait_dispatch`. With `PET_ENGINE` unset or anything but `v2`, the
first engine runs and the live brands are byte-identical to before this branch existed.

The animal is constructed from typography rather than a photograph with text laid over it:
streamlines follow the fur direction; each eye, the nose leather, the philtrum, the nostrils
and the mouth crease are drawn by dedicated passes from what is actually in the photo; the
silhouette sheds flyaway hairs; every remaining gap is filled down to the glyph floor. Letters
carry the photo's own colour; opacity carries tone. A type-only likeness score (face-weighted
SSIM of the blurred typography against the blurred source, no photo underneath) is printed
per render and was the judge for every change.

## The claim, and the numbers behind it

`PET_V2_VERBOSE=1` prints a `CLAIM METRICS` line per render. On the staging senior-dog upload:

    footprint 85.6% of the animal · exposed space 0.9% · 10,618 words
    letter-body collisions 1.08% · type-only likeness 0.572

On the four development photos at 1050px (`c835642`): black lab 0.618, tan dog 0.459,
doodle 0.408, cat 0.401; collisions 0.6–0.9%; exposed space 1.8–2.8%.

## What was found

**One size per streamline hid the whole hierarchy.** Each line took the average of the size
field over its full length. On a close-up a line runs cheek to frame edge, so every line
averaged to the same value: median 6px in every distance band. Words are now sized at their own
position. `945eb5a`.

**The "keep it fine" zone was the likeness weight map.** Its face ellipse is 1.8 eye-separations
wide. On a close-up it covered half the animal and 80% of structural words. Replaced by an
anatomical zone: eye discs, the fitted nose leather, a mouth band, with a short ramp. `945eb5a`,
widened for the eyes in `c835642`.

**Lanes were spaced for fine type.** Larger words on the far body collided and were rejected;
6px channel letters took the ground (120 structural words against 901 channel letters in the
far band). Lane spacing now floors at 0.85x the local type size. `945eb5a`.

**Distance from the face was distance from between the eyes.** On a close-up the nose is a full
eye-separation from that point, so the muzzle got the largest words in the frame. Distance is
now to the nearest feature, eye or nose, split into an anatomical near part (eye-separation
units) and a frame-relative far part. `c835642`.

**The engine kept per-render state in module globals.** Two renders overlapping on the service
threads shared one placement log; staging surfaced it to the customer as
`boolean index did not match indexed array ... 8767 vs 8761`. All per-render state is now
thread-local. Concurrent renders verified byte-identical to sequential. `a373443`.

**The backdrop choice was accepted and ignored**, and every request was a full render: preview,
loupe and each backdrop toggle overlapped and the third passed the browser's 120s limit. Now one
render per (photo, words, aspect) at the loupe height, cached; the loupe is a resize, a
backdrop toggle is arithmetic on the outer-ground weight, a duplicate request waits for the
render in flight. Measured: Gallery Gray 0.1s, print 64s, preview after print 0.1s. `12585cc`.

**Curvature-adaptive word length failed twice** (one long word dominated). Reverted to plain
round-robin; noted in the code.

## To deploy

    cd /root/typortrait-prod && git fetch origin claude/pet-engine-v2 && git checkout c835642
    cd /root/typortrait-stg/typography_engine
    TAG=$(SRC=/root/typortrait-prod ./ops/build-image.sh c835642) && echo "TAG=$TAG"
    ./ops/promote.sh typortrait-stg "$TAG" && ./staging.sh ps

An empty `TAG` means the build failed; do not promote. Production would be the same steps
against `typortrait-pawsinwords` with `PET_ENGINE=v2` in that tree's `.env`, after a soak.

Knobs: `PET_ENGINE`, `PET_V2_VERBOSE`, `PET_V2_MAX_RENDER_PX` (default 2400), `GOP_MAX_OVERLAP`
(default 0.08), `PET_V2_CACHE_ENTRIES` (default 4), `PET_V2_KEEP_FIELDS` (measurement only).

## What remains

1. The site's Small/Medium/Large type slider (`type_scale`) is accepted and ignored by v2.
2. `pet_landmarks.py` (RTMPose AP-10K) is not wired to the `landmarks` argument; the heuristic
   eye/nose detector is doing the work and has been right on every test photo so far.
3. The print render for `/download` runs inline (64s at 2400px); it should be a background job
   behind the "preparing files" state.
4. Type-only likeness fell 0.02–0.035 with the finer features in `c835642`; the customer's eye
   on staging decides whether that trade stands.
5. A regression gate through `ops/render-testset.sh` for the pet set, then the production
   promote.

The measurement scripts used here (`size_bands.py`, `zone_sizes.py`, `regset.py`) lived in the
session scratchpad and are not in the tree; the engine exposes what they need
(`_PLACEMENTS`, `_PLACEMENT_PASS`, `_FIELDS` with `PET_V2_KEEP_FIELDS=1`).
